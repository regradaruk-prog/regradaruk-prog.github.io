Free checklist. General information, not a guarantee of entitlement or a substitute for DVLA instructions.

Renew your entitlement

Check the expiry date on your V750 or V778. Use the official renewal service before expiry. Keep your contact details current.

DVLA renewal guidance

The file creates an event 30 days before the date you enter (or today if sooner). Import it into your calendar and check notifications yourself. No email or date is sent to us.

Returning a leased vehicle

Check the leasing company's process and arrange removal of the private registration before handback. Confirm the replacement registration and paperwork with the company.

Selling or scrapping a vehicle

Check eligibility and complete retention or transfer before disposing of the vehicle. Do not assume you can recover the registration afterwards.

DVLA removal guidance

Inherited registration

Establish who has authority to deal with the estate and contact DVLA for the applicable process before offering the registration for sale. Do not share certificate references or images publicly.
