# RegRadar — working business plan

Status: 25 November 2026. Working brand pending trademark clearance.

## Mission
UK-first independent buyer-led private registration discovery and classifieds marketplace. Free search and standard listings; zero sales commission; optional promotions and dealer tools later. No stock, no handling sale funds. Authorised data only. International-ready architecture; low overhead and automation-first operations. Aspirational mature owner workload 1–3 hours/week, not a guarantee.

## Guardrails
No spending, production publication, third-party contact or live customer data collection without owner approval. Work on development branches; review before merge. No certificate reference numbers or document photos. Human review of legal, fraud and moderation exceptions. Public repository: never commit secrets, personal data or confidential partner terms.

## Current baseline
Static GitHub Pages site with format-based suggestions, external links, local saved plates, manual comparable calculator and downloadable buyer/seller drafts. No verified inventory or live submission backend confirmed. Research agents cover inventory, marketing, strategy, technology and international expansion.

## First milestones (targets, not achieved)
100 genuine buyers, 100 private sellers, 100 authorised listings, first revenue. No additional expenditure approved.

## Build sequence
1. Audit existing search and accessibility; preserve current design.
2. Define structured buyer requests and seller listings, validation and matching; use synthetic test fixtures only.
3. Evaluate free-tier backend, server-side validation, verification, rate limits, private contact storage and moderation. Do not provision or collect live data until approved.
4. Pilot authorised listings and consent-based introductions; verify price completeness and freshness.
5. Add alerts, authorised imports and optional fixed-fee promotions only after evidence of demand.

## Unresolved gates
Trademark clearance; UK GDPR/privacy and Online Safety Act applicability; HMRC platform reporting scope; data licences; fraud controls; dealer willingness to pay; backend cost and limits. UK aircraft resale excluded from initial scope; other adjacent markets research only. No public claims of verified availability without evidence.
