# Proposed commercial operating rules — draft
Free search, standard listings and standard alerts. No transaction commission or custody of purchase funds. Optional fixed-price business services only after validation and approval. Clearly identify sponsored placement and affiliate links; preserve organic relevance and show complete known mandatory prices. No automatic paid enrolment. Define qualified lead, duplicate/refund criteria, monthly expenditure cap, cancellation and invoice rules before billing. Test willingness to pay with consenting dealers before building billing. Do not sell raw personal data. No price commitments, partner contact or payments authorised by this document.
