# Data rights and launch checklist — draft
Default deny for external inventory imports. Document the source, rights holder, permission evidence, permitted fields, purposes (display, search, matching, alerts, analytics), territories, expiry, update cadence, attribution and withdrawal mechanism. Check the supplier can license upstream data. Do not copy photos, descriptions, logos or databases without appropriate rights. Disable expired/revoked feeds and remove affected listings. Do not interpret public access or robots.txt as permission.
Dealer agreement outline (not a contract): non-exclusive scoped licence, accuracy and entitlement assurances, price/fee freshness, correction and takedown, termination, privacy responsibilities, applicable law, liability review. 
Review existing outgoing links against source terms; prefer official general guidance until permission for restricted integrations is resolved. No automated DVLA extraction. Review fonts and other third-party assets. Obtain name clearance before brand investment.
