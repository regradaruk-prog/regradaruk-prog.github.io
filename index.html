How buying a private plate works

  1. Pick a spelling

    Shorter, clearer plates are worth more. Dateless plates fit a car of any age.

  1. Check price and buy

    Buy from a reputable dealer, private seller or the DVLA directly. The price includes the right to the registration.

  1. Put it on your car

    Follow the DVLA assignment or transfer process. Then get physical plates made by a registered supplier.

Questions

Why are some letters shown as numbers?

Plates follow fixed UK formats, so names are often spelled using numbers that look like letters, such as 4 for A or 5 for S. Plates must still be displayed with the correct font and spacing by law; changing them can lead to a fine and the DVLA withdrawing the registration.

Can any plate go on my car?

Dateless plates have no age identifier, but DVLA eligibility rules still apply. Other styles carry an age identifier, and a plate can't make a vehicle look newer than it is.

Does RegRadar sell plates?

No. We help you discover possible spellings and prepare enquiries. We do not currently receive referral fees.

Is a plate available just because it's shown here?

No. We generate a selection of format-based spellings; some may never have been issued or may already be owned. The dealer or DVLA search tells you if it's for sale and the price.

Do plates make good gifts?

Yes. Most dealers offer a gift certificate so the person can assign the plate to their own car later.
