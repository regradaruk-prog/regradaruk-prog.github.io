How buying a private plate works

  1. Pick a spelling

    Shorter, clearer plates are worth more. Dateless plates fit a car of any age.

  1. Check price and buy

    Buy from a dealer registered with the DVLA, or from the DVLA directly. The price includes the right to the registration.

  1. Put it on your car

    The seller handles the DVLA paperwork. Then get physical plates made by a registered supplier.

Questions

Why are some letters shown as numbers?

Plates follow fixed UK formats, so names are often spelled using numbers that look like letters, such as 4 for A or 5 for S. Plates must still be displayed with the correct font and spacing by law; changing them can lead to a fine and the DVLA withdrawing the registration.

Can any plate go on my car?

Dateless plates can go on any vehicle. Other styles carry an age identifier, and a plate can't make a vehicle look newer than it is.

Does RegRadar sell plates?

No. We help you find spellings and send you to dealers who sell them. We may earn a referral fee from them if you buy, at no extra cost to you.

Is a plate available just because it's shown here?

No. We show every valid spelling; many are already owned. The dealer or DVLA search tells you if it's for sale and the price.

Do plates make good gifts?

Yes. Most dealers offer a gift certificate so the person can assign the plate to their own car later.
